How to Use

  1. Enter or paste your text.
  2. Choose a language and an available voice.
  3. Adjust speed and pitch if needed.
  4. Press Speak Text to hear the result.

About This Tool

TTS Real Human Voices is a browser-based text-to-speech tool. It uses the speech synthesis voices available on your device or browser.

Voice quality, language support and voice availability depend on your device, browser and installed speech services. Internet access may be required for some voices.

This basic version does not upload your text to a dedicated AI voice service or generate downloadable MP3 files.

Privacy

This webpage does not contain code that sends the text you enter to a server. Speech is requested through the browser's speech synthesis interface. Device and browser speech services may process speech requests according to their own privacy practices.
